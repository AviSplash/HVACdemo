Our story

From one truck to a Texas-wide team

In 2008, founder Ray Delgado was tired of watching big HVAC outfits scare families into repairs they didn't need. He started Atlas Air & Heat with a single van, a toolbox, and one rule: treat every home like it's your mama's.

Eighteen years later, that rule still runs the company. We've grown to a fleet of fully-stocked trucks and a roster of NATE-certified technicians serving Greater Houston — but we've never traded the straight talk and fair pricing that got us here.

When the Texas sun is relentless, our neighbors don't have time for guesswork. They need comfort restored, fast and honestly. That's the Atlas promise, on every single call.
